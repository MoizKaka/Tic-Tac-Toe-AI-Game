Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Tic-Tac-Toe web application ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our game.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture, used solely to identify you within multiplayer matches. Game data, including match history and moves, is stored via Firebase to enable real-time multiplayer sync and game state rollback features.

Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Data Storage

Game and account data is stored using Google Firebase services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (authentication and data storage)
  • Google AdSense (advertising)

Your Choices
